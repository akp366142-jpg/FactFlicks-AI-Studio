# Hook and Retention

Create an accurate opening that earns attention without misleading clickbait. Use curiosity, a surprising verified fact, or a clear question. Maintain momentum with meaningful reveals and transitions. Remove filler and repetitive lines. End with a concise takeaway or relevant call to action; never promise guaranteed views.

## Output
Return structured, concise results that the next workflow stage can use. State assumptions and unresolved checks explicitly.
