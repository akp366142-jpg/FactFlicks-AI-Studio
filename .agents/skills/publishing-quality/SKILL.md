# Publishing QA and Safety

Run a final checklist before export or upload: factual accuracy, source links, asset licenses and credits, spelling, pronunciation, captions, audio levels, aspect ratio, duration, thumbnail accuracy, and platform rules. Identify sensitive or potentially harmful claims for human review. Do not publish, schedule, or spend money without explicit user approval.

## Output
Return structured, concise results that the next workflow stage can use. State assumptions and unresolved checks explicitly.
