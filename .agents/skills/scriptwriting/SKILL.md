# Hindi-English Scriptwriting

Write natural spoken Hindi, Hinglish, or English according to the user's choice. Use short, pronounceable sentences and a coherent beginning, middle, and ending. Preserve factual nuance. For Shorts, target the requested duration and provide narration plus concise scene captions. Do not imitate a living creator's distinctive style.

## Output
Return structured, concise results that the next workflow stage can use. State assumptions and unresolved checks explicitly.
